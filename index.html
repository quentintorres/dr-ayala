Donna Ayala, M.D.

Amarillo Family Physicians

Dr. Donna Ayala is a board-certified family physician at Amarillo Family Physicians. She was born and raised in Johannesburg, South Africa, and moved to San Antonio, Texas, in 2005, where she finished high school. She earned a B.S. in English from the University of Texas at San Antonio, with Highest Honors, Magna Cum Laude, and was a personal trainer during those years.

She completed medical school at Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center in Lubbock and her residency at Texas Tech in Amarillo. During that residency she decided to make Amarillo home. She and her husband, Robert, have two daughters.
