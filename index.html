Her story

From Johannesburg to Amarillo.

Dr. Donna Ayala is a board certified family physician at Amarillo Family Physicians. She came to Amarillo for residency and decided to make it home.

Details from her practice profile.

  1. 01

    Johannesburg, South Africa

    Born and raised.

    She moved to Texas in 2005 and finished high school in San Antonio.

  3. 03

    University of Texas at San Antonio

    B.S. in English, with Highest Honors, Magna Cum Laude. She was a personal trainer during those years.

  4. 04

    Lubbock, Texas

    Medical school at Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center School of Medicine.

  5. 05

    Amarillo, Texas

    Residency at Texas Tech in Amarillo, the city she chose to call home.
